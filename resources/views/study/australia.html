Study in Australia Overview

Why Study Here?

Australia offers world-class education, excellent post-study work rights (up to 4-6 years), and a clear pathway to permanent residency. Its multicultural environment is incredibly welcoming for Nepalese students.

Cost Overview

Tuition: AUD $25,000 - $40,000/year. Living Expenses: ~AUD $24,500/year.

Intake Information

Major Intakes: February and July. (Some universities offer November intakes).

Visa Difficulty & Process

The Subclass 500 Student Visa process requires strong financial documentation, English proficiency (IELTS/PTE), and a Genuine Student (GS) assessment.

Admission Requirements

Academic Scores

Minimum 60% in previous academics (Class 12 / Bachelor's).

English Proficiency

IELTS 6.0 - 6.5 overall, or equivalent PTE scores.

Financial Capacity

Proof of sufficient funds for 1 year of tuition and living.
