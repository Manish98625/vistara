Study in USA Overview

Why Study Here?

The USA offers unparalleled academic flexibility, cutting-edge technology, and abundant OPT/CPT opportunities for practical training. It is the top destination for STEM students.

Cost Overview

Tuition: USD $20,000 - $55,000/year. Living Expenses: ~USD $15,000 - $20,000/year.

Intake Information

Major Intakes: Fall (August/September) and Spring (January).

Visa Difficulty & Process

The F-1 Student Visa process involves the I-20 form and an in-person embassy interview. Our mock interview preparation ensures you face the visa officer with complete confidence.

Admission Requirements

Academic Scores

Strong GPA. Standardized tests (SAT/ACT/GRE/GMAT) may be required depending on the university.

English Proficiency

IELTS 6.0 - 7.0, TOEFL iBT 79+, or Duolingo English Test (DET) 105+.

Financial Capacity

Bank statement showing funds for 1 year of tuition and living expenses to receive I-20.
